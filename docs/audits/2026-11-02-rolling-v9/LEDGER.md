## Round summary (2026-11-02)

All eight areas were audited, refuted and re-verified. 68 findings were raised and none was refuted. Result: P1 16 / P2 21 / P3 31, plus 38 KNOWN.

**What holds.** Every v8 finding is fixed on the case its report gave, and nearly every regression test fails on its parent. The whole-job journeys for an image sign, an old project and a remote edit came through clean. A project saved by 1.0.28 burns the same on main in 425 of 449 comparisons. The flaky `in-window-stall-beam` test has a demonstrated cause in the Marlin test model.

**Tracer against Potrace 1.16.** Line Art 51 / 20 / 8, Sharp 49 / 22 / 8, Smooth 55 / 17 / 7 over 79 images, confirmed by a separate scorer; after JPEG q75 Smooth rose to 54 / 17 / 8. Better up to about 200 px, glyphs up to 48 px, and anti-aliased art at every size measured. Not better on curved 1-bit art from about 500 px, 1-bit glyphs from 96 px, hard mid-grey art on Line Art, or specific text marks. Sharp's lead depends on the corner metrics (38 / 14 / 27 without them).

**Regressions from the fixes this round checked:** R2-P1-1 (#1517), R3-P1-2 (#1522), R4-P1-1 (#1523), R5-P1-1 (#1526), R6-P1-2 and R6-P2-1 (#1521). As in v8, each PR's tests covered what it reported. Most other P1s are sibling routes or older defects that fix verification reached for the first time. Lens L11 was the most productive new check: R7-P1-1 is a harm already in the field on 1.0.28.

**Fix status at round end.** Merged: #1533, #1534, #1535, #1536. Open: #1537, #1538, #1539. In a session: R2-P1-1, R2-P2-1, R2-P2-2, R2-P2-4. Cards: R6, R8. None of these has been re-audited, and no review bot has read them (Codex at its limit, CodeRabbit skips small repositories).
