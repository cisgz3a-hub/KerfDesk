## Status (2026-11-03): paused after S3

The maintainer asked for a different loop (new-user experience) and chose to finish S3 first. S1–S3 are audited, refuted and re-verified: 19 findings raised, none refuted; P1 2 / P2 8 / P3 9, plus 10 KNOWN. S4–S9 are still to do, from the same plan.

- **Fixed:** S1-P1-1 (#1547), S1-P2-1 (#1548), S2-P2-1 and S2-P2-2 (#1552), S2-P2-3 and S2-P2-4 (#1551).
- **Open:** S1-P2-2 (Start preparation; S4 to measure), and S3-P2-1..3 (fix cards proposed).
- **So far:** at ordinary sizes (a few hundred objects, a normal job) KerfDesk meets every budget; the misses are on large jobs (hundreds of thousands of lines), dense scenes (5,000+ objects) and bed-sized photos.
