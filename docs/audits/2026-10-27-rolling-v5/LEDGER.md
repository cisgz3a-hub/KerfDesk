## Round summary (2026-10-28)

All ten areas were audited, refuted and re-verified in code by the coordinator. 43 findings were raised, and none was refuted outright. The result is P1 9 / P2 15 / P3 19, with 63 KNOWN items re-observed.

**Fix verification (J1, J3, J5, J7, J9).** Every v3/v4 fix PR fixed the path its finding reported. Their regression tests fail on the pre-fix code. The misses are siblings and regressions:
- the tile header below line 4 (J1);
- remote SVG on the UI thread (J3);
- the bed-only notice hiding other machine changes (J5);
- the Smoothieware `G21` regression and FluidNC brackets under a GRBL profile (J7);
- Array on board dropping a photo's mask (J9).

**App-wide lenses (J2, J4, J6, J8, J10):**
- **J2, failure paths:** laser left on at Idle, crash-screen notices, stale tiles; all fixed.
- **J4, trust boundaries:** clean.
- **J6, adversarial parsers:**
  - two scale P1s;
  - two security-held items, fixed and lifted;
  - a UI-thread decode.
- **J8, catalogue ground truth:** 40 values verified, 0 wrong. One P1: the Falcon A1 Pro `$150`–`$152` are microstep settings.
- **J10, scale:** the job-time safety paths hold under load. The two P2s are large-selection edits and the save size limit.
