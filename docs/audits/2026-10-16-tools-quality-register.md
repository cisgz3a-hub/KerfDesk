## How to read this list

- **Design/research:** every row was reviewed against its source, relevant product contracts and the primary references in its linked group report. A citation supports a design criterion; it does not certify the implementation.
- **Automated evidence:** model, geometry, worker, store and component assertions. A passing guard test is not a successful maximum-size operation. Mocked provider/font/trace orchestration is identified in the detailed report.
- **Browser workflow:** actual DOM interactions in Chrome, with synthetic project fixtures where stated. These checks are separate from direct computer-use interaction and installed desktop qualification.
- **Direct computer use:** `Done` means the agent completed the stated representative workflow through the computer-use tool. `Partial` means the stated prerequisite or intermediate view was checked, but the complete workflow was not. `Unavailable` identifies the unimplemented command. These are workflow observations, not universal correctness certificates.
- **Stress:** the precise bounded workload or gap is stated. No row implicitly claims unlimited size, endurance, memory or hardware qualification.
