## Round summary (2026-10-28)

Three follow-up areas were audited, refuted and re-verified in main's code. 13 findings were raised and none was refuted. Result: P1 4 / P2 4 / P3 5, plus 15 KNOWN.

- **K1: a UI stall on the window transport leaves the beam lit on a stopped head.** Three P1s: Marlin fan, Marlin inline, and the GRBL family under M3. This was the mechanism earlier refuters had noted but never raised.
- **K2: watchdogs under UI blocks.** USB is clean. Three P2s on the network transport and the Pause/Resume deadline: false or abrupt stops that are dark and announced.
- **K3: Falcon A1 Pro preset against two published dumps.** CoreXY acceleration gives too short a runway (P1). The preset says homing is off while the firmware homes (P2). A read-only checklist for the maintainer's own A1 Pro was verified safe.

The same branch also marks every confirmed P1/P2 from rolling v5 and quality audit Q1 as fixed: #1486–#1497.
