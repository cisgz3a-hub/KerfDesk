| K1-P1-1 | K1 | **Marlin fan-mosfet laser: a UI stall leaves the beam on at burn power over a stopped head for the rest of the stall.** Marlin always streams from the window (ping-pong, refilled only by the window's `ok` handler), so any UI-thread stall longer than the queued motion (about 0.1–0.8 s) drains the planner; the fan output keeps its last speed when the planner empties and has no firmware timeout. Refuter repro (own controller model on a worker thread, real store, real busy-loop block): a 2,300 ms block drains in 127 ms and leaves fan 204/255 on a stopped head for 2,173 ms; on a hung window it stays lit until the physical E-stop. No warning; the job resumes afterwards with no notice. Real stalls of 0.1–3 s exist (J10) | **READ** (main): `serialOpenRequest` asks for the worker only when `isGrblFamilyDriver(driver)` (`laser-connect-action.ts:300-305`; `['grbl-v1.1','grblhal','fluidnc']`, `laser-disconnect-transaction.ts:41-45`); `marlin-fan` sets power with `M106 S`/`M107` between moves (`gcode-dialects.ts:38-44`). **SOURCE**: Marlin 2.1.2.8 fan keeps its speed when the planner empties. Auditor + refuter REPRO | fix card proposed |
