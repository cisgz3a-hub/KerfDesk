## Reproduction and acceptance details

### F01: Paint, fill and erase force upper-layer pixels opaque

Trigger: New transparent upper layer, red Pencil at 50% opacity or Fill with mask alpha128, then Eraser.

Observed: Paint/Fill produces [128,0,0,255], expected [255,0,0,128]; composite dark red instead of pink. Eraser writes opaque color instead of revealing lower layer.

Correction: Destination-alpha-aware source-over paint/fill and layer-aware destination-out eraser. Preserve selected Background color from PR729 and history/cache/selection behavior.

- Fractional paint/fill onto transparent and semitransparent layers retain correct RGBA/composite.
- Upper eraser reveals lower layer; Background honors PR729 chosen color.
- Undo/Redo, preview and Apply retain the same pixel result.

### F02: Merge Down changes artwork on transparent lower layer

Trigger: White Background, transparent lower layer, opaque red upper at50% layer opacity; Merge Down.

Observed: Before composite [255,128,128,255], after [128,0,0,255].

Correction: Merge normal layers with straight RGBA and both effective opacities. Bake non-normal combinations against their current backdrop into a normal merged layer, retaining effective alpha and visibility.

- Composite equivalence before/after normal merges over several lower backgrounds.
- Partial pixel alpha, non-unit upper/lower opacity, hidden layers and supported blend cases.
- Preserve lower identity and documented history/cache invalidation.

### F03: Off-image rectangle marquee selects unrelated pixels

Trigger: 10x10 image; rectangle x=-20,y=1,width=5,height=3 wholly left of image.

Observed: 85 pixels selected, indices10..94; expected0. Negative TypedArray.fill end wraps from buffer end.

Correction: Intersect both pixel-center spans with image bounds and return empty before fill for no overlap.

- Fully outside each edge selects0.
- Partial/fractional rectangles match exact intersecting pixel centers.
- Pointer completion plus existing selection boolean modes remain correct.

### F04: Editor Crop fabricates wrong pixels and physical bounds outside image

Trigger: 10x10 image; crop x12,y1,w3,h3 outside, or x-2,y1,w5,h3 partially left.

Observed: Outside crop becomes1x3 at physical x12..13; partial-left becomes5x3 instead of3x3. Linear copy may read another source row.

Correction: Compute one correct rectangle intersection before pixel copy; no overlap is no-op, never force fabricated1px.

- No-overlap crops preserve content/revision/history/bounds.
- Partial/fractional/repeated crops use exact pixels and physical mapping.
- All layers, pending Apply and Revert remain consistent.

### F05: Image Size moves and stretches cropped artwork

Trigger: 10px/10mm image, crop x/y2..5 =>3x3px, resize to2x2px.

Observed: Bounds2..5 become1.6666667..5; rounded base6 and offset1 expand3mm to3.333mm and move left/down.

Correction: Keep continuous physical pixel size and crop offsets independent of rounded Revert-base dimensions. Apply restores the original physical bounds after Revert as well as preserving cropped bounds after Image Size.

- Odd/noninteger/repeated/anisotropic Image Size after crop preserves exact bounds.
- Canvas Size padding and later crops consistent.
- Revert, source transforms and scene Apply preserve intended geometry.
- Crop, Apply, Revert and Apply restores original scene bounds; Apply of a resized crop preserves its physical extent.

### F06: Odd-size downsampling drops trailing row and column

Trigger: 5x5 white source with black final row and column, downsample2x2.

Observed: All9 black edge pixels disappear; output entirelywhite. floor-based halve never reads index4.

Correction: Cover entire source domain including odd trailing samples with appropriate alpha-aware filtering at each reduction.

- 5x5->2x2 border/corner contribution retained.
- Mirror/rotation-equivalent fixtures stay equivalent.
- Flat fields, large odd reductions and transparent edges retain correct target dimensions.

### F380-01: CNC profile leads can cross and cut a neighboring kept part

Trigger: Two disjoint outside-profile contours with an accepted line lead whose endpoints lie outside the neighboring contour but whose segment crosses it. The probe uses a persisted supported profileLead shape=line, radiusMm=10 setting and default 3.175mm cutter.

Observed: cncGroupForLayer emits a path3d lead at Z=-1 from (38.413,60.0005) to (48.413,60.0005), through an actual kept 3x3mm sibling at X41.913..44.913,Y58.5005..61.5005. G-code contains G1 X48.413 Y60.001 Z-1.000 F1000 after the waste-side plunge.

Correction: Check complete lead-in/out segments against existing kept-part geometry, including sibling and self-side crossings as applicable. Reuse the existing legacy-plunge fallback without adding an operational refusal or new policy gate.

- Line crossing with all endpoints outside falls back.
- Arc chord crossing with outside endpoints falls back.
- Clear leads retain geometry and emitted bytes.
- Full compiler/emitter regression, repeated depth, hole/rough-finish identity, and physical origin cases remain correct.

### F351-01: Paint Bucket overwrites pixels outside the active selection

Trigger: Open a uniform image, select a small rectangle, and click Paint Bucket inside it.

Observed: An 8x1 white document with only its first 2 pixels selected becomes [0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0] after black bucket fill.

Correction: Intersect the wand region with the active selection, preserving feather alpha, then derive history/dirty bounds from the effective mask. Preserve full-region behavior with no active selection.

- Hard selection limits paint.
- Feather alpha controls blended paint.
- Clicks outside the selection do not paint unselected pixels.
- Multilayer sampling and one-step undo/redo remain correct.

### F353-01: Single-layer Clone Stamp smears its own new pixels

Trigger: On an ordinary one-layer image, clone along a stroke whose destination overlaps its source.

Observed: The 8x1 ramp [20,40,60,80,100,120,140,160], cloned rightward with offset(-1,0), becomes all 20.

Correction: Ensure the clone source is independent of its destination before mutation. Copy when the composite aliases the destination; avoid copying a composite that is already independent.

- Overlapping strokes in both directions use pre-stroke pixels.
- One-layer and multilayer paths agree.
- Selection, opacity, aligned offset, and undo/redo remain correct.

Correction: Use physical raster dimensions after absolute object scale for the estimate, following the actual scan-axis contract. Keep ink coverage independent of physical size.

- Uniform and nonuniform scale change duration appropriately.
- Negative mirror scales retain positive duration.
- Cropped document size and per-object settings retain the current intended estimate model.
