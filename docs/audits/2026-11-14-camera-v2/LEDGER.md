# Camera audit v2 — ledger

Plan: [AREAS.md](AREAS.md). Severity follows [METHOD.md §3](../2026-10-21-rolling-v2/METHOD.md):
- **P1:** harms a job, loses work or exposes a secret, or lets such a defect reach `main` unchecked. There is no reach clause, and a visible result is not a warning. For the camera, this includes artwork placed off the material under a "good" grade with no warning.
- **P2:** a real defect with limited harm. A test gap behind correct code is P2 at most.
- **P3:** structural.

## Round result (2026-11-14)

**Totals:** 31 raised, 0 refuted, P1 10, P2 10, P3 11, 45 KNOWN, across K1–K5. Every finding was reproduced by an auditor and an independent refuter with their own truth cameras or stubs, and every P1/P2 was re-read in main's code.

**What the round found.** The camera's maths is sound where it is measured: on the calibration sheet the figures are honest, the overlay draws within 0.01 mm of the burn on a flat bed, every tool is exact on simple blanks, the camera never slows a job, and logins never reach a saved or shared file. The serious findings are about trusting what lies outside that measurement:
- **Accuracy claimed past the rings:** the bed estimate read 2–3× low for centred webcams, and Fix hid a tilt (K1).
- **A picture that is no longer true:** a live camera checked once per start, and a rotary picture drawn at the wrong height (K2).
- **What counts as a piece:** a two-way colour split that turns glare, shadows and scorch into ticked pieces, copies placed off the material, and a calibration target burned off its layout under Print and Cut (K3).
- **A fragile recording:** a second-long hiccup or a closed RTSP preview ended the timelapse (K4).

**Fixed on main:** #1917 and #1918 (K1). **Open fix PRs:** #1922 (K2 and K1-P2-2), #1925 (K3), #1930 (K4). **Routed:** the K5 security finding to the camera session.

**Maintainer decisions:**
- K2-P2-1: should Material height follow the job's material thickness and be saved (ADR-441 Amd 2 §5 chose "not saved")?
- K4-P2-3: should the next run keep the last timelapse until it is saved or dismissed (ADR-490)?
- K5-P3-3: should a network camera's calibration carry to another computer for the same camera and address?

**Follow-ups:** a check at the moment of Start for a camera-placed job (K2-P1-1's last gap); automatic reconnect of a failed RTSP camera (K4-P2-2); the frozen last picture of snapshot and phone cameras (W4-P3-1); a quiet-machine run of large snapshot frames against the G-code stream; the default part sheet's "about 3–4 mm" grade on mild lenses (#1917's trade-off).

**Structural direction.** Grade the camera by what was measured and say what was not; re-check a picture before it is trusted for a burn; segment pieces against the bed's own reference rather than a two-way split; and treat a recording as the user's work.
