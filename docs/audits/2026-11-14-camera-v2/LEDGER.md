# Camera audit v2 — ledger

Plan: [AREAS.md](AREAS.md). Severity follows [METHOD.md §3](../2026-10-21-rolling-v2/METHOD.md):
- **P1:** harms a job, loses work or exposes a secret, or lets such a defect reach `main` unchecked. There is no reach clause, and a visible result is not a warning. For the camera, this includes artwork placed off the material under a "good" grade with no warning.
- **P2:** a real defect with limited harm. A test gap behind correct code is P2 at most.
- **P3:** structural.

### P3s (8)

- **K1r-P3-1 (refuter):** the full-bed sheet the app recommends is refused ("anchors-unclear") on a mildly soft 1280×960 webcam: four far-row rings read as solid discs. A safe refusal, but the message never suggests a larger ring size; 16 mm rings calibrate (36/36).
- **K2-P3-1:** the moved check reports per-point offsets with the opposite sign to its documentation (the negative of the true displacement in all 13 cases); only sizes are used today.
- **K2r-P3-1 (refuter):** a mirrored picture can be measured as an ordinary move; it warns with the right advice, but the stated size is about 18 times too small (24.3 mm against a true 437 mm).
- **K3-P3-1:** a piece 2 mm or less from the bed edge, fully in view, is marked "Partly out of the camera's view".
- **K3-P3-2:** an L-shaped offcut is called round and listed as "⌀ 120.5 mm".
- **K4-P3-1:** a calibrated recording silently stops adding frames if the camera changes picture size mid-job, and then fetches pictures 5–7 times as often as planned (READ by the refuter).
- **K4-P3-2:** a cut JPEG passes the bridge only when the camera sends no length or a length matching the cut bytes, and is then kept as a frame (Chromium decode not verified).
- **K4r-P3-1 (refuter):** two views asking for the same camera share one fetch, but its failure is counted once per request, so one failure holds the camera back 2 s (4 s with three views); this drives the overlay case of K4-P2-1.

### Verified clean

- **On the target sheet the figures are honest:** true worst error 0.06–0.57 mm wherever no ring was deliberately bad (one strong tangential lens at 0.84 mm is already graded fair).
- **Check camera** flagged every move tried (8 of 8, both models): slides, tilts, rolls, a lowered lid.
- **Bad rings** (swapped labels, rings 4 px or more off, rings engraved 2.5–6 mm off) are dropped; saved calibrations reload bit-exact; blur, a half-covered target and over-wide fisheyes are refused rather than misfitted.
- **Material height** is exact when the camera height is pinned (about +0.5 mm at 40 mm).
- **KNOWN:** Y1-P2-1 is fixed by #1684 for off-centre cameras but still reachable for centred ones (K1-P1-1); Y1-P3-3 (σ 1.0 px blur blamed on a covered target) still reproduces; #1546 items 1, 2, 3 and 6 are still open.
