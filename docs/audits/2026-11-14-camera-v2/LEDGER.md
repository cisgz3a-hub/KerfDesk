# Camera audit v2 — ledger

Plan: [AREAS.md](AREAS.md). Severity follows [METHOD.md §3](../2026-10-21-rolling-v2/METHOD.md):
- **P1:** harms a job, loses work or exposes a secret, or lets such a defect reach `main` unchecked. There is no reach clause, and a visible result is not a warning. For the camera, this includes artwork placed off the material under a "good" grade with no warning.
- **P2:** a real defect with limited harm. A test gap behind correct code is P2 at most.
- **P3:** structural.

### P3s (15)

- **K1r-P3-1 (refuter):** the full-bed sheet the app recommends is refused ("anchors-unclear") on a mildly soft 1280×960 webcam: four far-row rings read as solid discs. A safe refusal, but the message never suggests a larger ring size; 16 mm rings calibrate (36/36).
- **K2-P3-1:** the moved check reports per-point offsets with the opposite sign to its documentation (the negative of the true displacement in all 13 cases); only sizes are used today.
- **K2r-P3-1 (refuter):** a mirrored picture can be measured as an ordinary move; it warns with the right advice, but the stated size is about 18 times too small (24.3 mm against a true 437 mm).
- **K3-P3-1:** a piece 2 mm or less from the bed edge, fully in view, is marked "Partly out of the camera's view".
- **K3-P3-2:** an L-shaped offcut is called round and listed as "⌀ 120.5 mm".
- **K4-P3-1:** a calibrated recording silently stops adding frames if the camera changes picture size mid-job, and then fetches pictures 5–7 times as often as planned (READ by the refuter).
- **K4-P3-2:** a cut JPEG passes the bridge only when the camera sends no length or a length matching the cut bytes, and is then kept as a frame (Chromium decode not verified).
- **K4r-P3-1 (refuter):** two views asking for the same camera share one fetch, but its failure is counted once per request, so one failure holds the camera back 2 s (4 s with three views); this drives the overlay case of K4-P2-1.
- **K5-P3-2:** an https camera with its own certificate, or one that redirects, can never connect, and the message reads like an unplugged camera ("check the address") instead of naming the cause.
- **K5-P3-3:** a network camera's calibration does not carry to another computer, even for the same camera at the same address, because the identity uses a per-computer key. **Maintainer decision.**
- **K5r-P3-4 (refuter):** on the same computer, a password change makes the calibration read as "a different camera" when the login sits in the address's query or path (a `user:password@` login keeps the match).
- **K6-P3-1:** a dark knot can be read as a hole, so that piece is left out with a wrong reason (a central knot instead goes through the neck cut, K6-P1-2).
- **K6-P3-2:** the rotary height rule accepts the part's diameter as its top (5.4 mm off).
- **K6-P3-3:** the cut-JPEG check refuses whole JPEGs with 15 or more bytes after the end marker.
- **K6-P3-4:** SECURITY (redacted): local denial of service, no login use (CWE-400); a residual after #1932.

## Round result (2026-11-14)

**Totals:** 31 raised, 0 refuted, P1 10, P2 10, P3 11, 45 KNOWN, across K1–K5. Every finding was reproduced by an auditor and an independent refuter with their own truth cameras or stubs, and every P1/P2 was re-read in main's code.

**What the round found.** The camera's maths is sound where it is measured: on the calibration sheet the figures are honest, the overlay draws within 0.01 mm of the burn on a flat bed, every tool is exact on simple blanks, the camera never slows a job, and logins never reach a saved or shared file. The serious findings are about trusting what lies outside that measurement:
- **Accuracy claimed past the rings:** the bed estimate read 2–3× low for centred webcams, and Fix hid a tilt (K1).
- **A picture that is no longer true:** a live camera checked once per start, and a rotary picture drawn at the wrong height (K2).
- **What counts as a piece:** a two-way colour split that turns glare, shadows and scorch into ticked pieces, copies placed off the material, and a calibration target burned off its layout under Print and Cut (K3).
- **A fragile recording:** a second-long hiccup or a closed RTSP preview ended the timelapse (K4).

**Fixed on main:** #1917 and #1918 (K1). **Open fix PRs:** #1922 (K2 and K1-P2-2), #1925 (K3), #1930 (K4). **Routed:** the K5 security finding to the camera session.
