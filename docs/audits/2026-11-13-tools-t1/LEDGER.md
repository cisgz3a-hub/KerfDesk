Every Material Test cell burns exactly the F and S its labels state, with no stray burns and no label overlapping a cell. The October repair (fractional feeds such as 0.125 appear exactly in labels and operation names) still holds.

Every Interval Test swatch burns at the line interval its label states (0.116316 mm requested gives a mean decoded step of 0.116316 mm). The October label repair still holds.

Every Tone Calibration wedge patch burns within 0.17 percentage points of its commanded tone (Jarvis, 254 DPI); Grayscale S is exact on every patch, across `$30` 1000/255, a session override, Stucki, Floyd, Ordered, unidirectional, scan offset, 2 passes and two rotary types. The fitted curve agrees with an independent fit within one LUT step on 16 reading shapes (flat, reversed, NaN, out of range, a single reading, empty); untouched sliders give the identity, so they change no pixel. A calibrated 16-step grey ramp emits exactly the S the curve predicts, and a project round trip is byte-identical. A tampered non-monotonic curve is refused, not repaired.

The Scan Offset Test coupon burns as designed: baseline swatches run two-way at 0 mm, verification swatches move each row by half the table value at the swatch speed, interpolated between table points (linear from rest below the first, clamped above the last), and the measured values land in the profile table with the right sign and speed.

Focus Test moves Z safely: in all 29 decoded setups every Z move is relative and made with the laser off, G90 is restored before any XY move, output stays in mm, Z returns exactly to its start, and every line burns at the Z offset its name gives. Marlin, Smoothieware, Ruida, K40 and Moshi refuse it at compile (`focus-test-uncompilable`) instead of dropping the Z intent, which the October follow-up required of a real implementation.

Trace Image registers within one source pixel of the source's dark pixels in all 10 placements, after the T16/T17 mask fixes, and holes and islands burn correctly under Fill.
