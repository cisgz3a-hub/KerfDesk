### D2 verified clean

- **The G-code family is one choke point.** Six dialects (grbl 1.1, grblHAL, FluidNC, Marlin inline, Marlin fan, Smoothieware) give identical lit geometry, power shares, rotary feed, tab share, Fill scan offset and air words for the same job. The power mode differs by design and is announced in the layer editor.
- Every route shares the compile-time rules: origin corner, cut order, tab geometry, leads, Focus Test refusal, rotary A/Z refusal, compile-integrity refusals and bed and no-go preflight.
- D1's note that the `.rd` writer ignores dot width and scan offset is **refuted**: the `.rd` encoder refuses every Fill and Image layer.

### D2 structural direction

Most of D1 and D2 have one cause: the Moshiboard and Ruida network routes build their own option sets, planners and warnings instead of passing through the shared ones. The report recommends:
- one start-options builder for every route;
- job advisories computed in core behind `PreparedOutput`;
- encoders that only turn the shared planners' moves into bytes;
- one rotary feed-run helper;
- driver capabilities instead of per-encoder notes;
- one machine-work event bus.
