## Statutory disclosure questions remain separate

Paddle’s published domain checklist asks for product/pricing/features, accessible Terms/Refund/Privacy, a company name or sole proprietor’s brand/legal name, and a live HTTPS site. It does not list a licensor public address, telephone, lawyer, incorporated company or Windows code-signing certificate as a website requirement. Its Buyer Terms distinguish a purchase from Paddle from the product supplied under the software Supplier Agreement.

South Africa’s Electronic Communications and Transactions Act section 42 limits the consumer-protection chapter to electronic transactions. Section 43 requires physical address/telephone and legal-service address disclosures from an applicable supplier offering goods or services electronically. Applying that duty to the KerfDesk licensor alongside Paddle resale remains a legal scope question; neither calling Paddle merchant of record nor a passing website check establishes an exemption. The previous universal address/telephone publication gate was a conservative inference, not Paddle’s stated checklist.

There is also a distinct privacy question: POPIA section 18(1) requires reasonably practicable notice of the responsible party’s name/address, and applicable international-transfer/protection information, when collecting personal information. Its scope follows the actual responsible party and processing, not simply whether checkout is open. No exemption, sufficient alternative notification arrangement or established transfer compliance is asserted here.

The owner has no authorised public business/service address or public telephone arrangement. Do not infer or publish a residence/mobile, substitute Paddle’s address for the proprietor’s, or claim email alone satisfies every requirement. A company office or incorporation is not established as necessary. Any alternative service/contact arrangement must be genuine, authorised and checked against the applicable requirement. The existing studio label Ons Houtkombuis is not asserted as a separate legal seller entity.

The full PAIA draft has separate contact, physical inspection, access-record and provider-processing work. Actual Information Officer registration is not asserted from a template. PAIA is not a listed Paddle domain-review document, and the unfinished manual is excluded from public output.
