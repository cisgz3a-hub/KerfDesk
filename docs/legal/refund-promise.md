**We promise a full refund if you request it within 14 calendar days after purchase.** This applies to both the initial Pro purchase and an optional year of updates. You do not need to give a reason, and you can request it after activating and using Pro.

We may refuse a request supported by clear evidence of fraud, refund abuse or other manipulative behaviour, only to the extent the law permits. Using Pro during the refund period is not itself abuse.

This is KerfDesk’s additional promise. Paddle’s default discretionary refund policy does not replace it. Paddle’s Refund Policy recognises additional rights supplied by the software supplier and mandatory law. The highest applicable level of rights applies.

## 3. How to request a refund

Email **support@kerfdesk.com** within the applicable period. Tell us that you want a refund and provide the email used for the purchase and, if available, the Paddle receipt or order reference. You do not need to include a reason for a request within our 14-day period. Never send a full licence key or payment-card details.

You can also use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) and choose its refund request option. An eligible statutory withdrawal can also be requested through the options Paddle provides for your transaction.

We verify the purchase and promptly request the refund from Paddle when our promise applies. If Paddle declines a request covered by our promise, contact us so we can follow it up. Keep the receipt and a copy of your request; a timely request does not become late while it is being processed.

## 4. After 14 days and uncertain delivery

After our 14-day period, duplicate or incorrect charges, an undelivered or unusable licence, product defects and any other applicable legal refund or correction rights remain available. Contact us with the order reference and the problem so we can help or request the appropriate remedy from Paddle.

If a purchase appears incomplete, do not pay again to resolve uncertainty. Keep the receipt and recovery information and ask us to check the order. No trial or licence condition removes applicable consumer rights.

## 5. How the money is returned

A full refund covers the amount you paid for the refunded purchase, including the applicable tax. Paddle normally returns it to the original payment method where possible. Its published policy normally provides processing within 14 days after approval; statutory withdrawal and other mandatory refund deadlines apply where required.

How long the refund then takes to appear depends on the payment method and bank. Contact us or Paddle if the expected refund has not arrived. We do not replace a refund with store credit unless you choose that and the law permits it.

## 6. Effect on Pro rights

A full refund of the initial Pro purchase ends the Pro rights granted by that purchase. Free remains available, rights from other valid purchases remain, and no licence or refund action stops a running job.

A refund of an optional update year does **not** revoke your base Pro licence. We will correct the update coverage where that is supported safely. If we cannot remove the added coverage without taking away your base rights, you keep that extra coverage without being charged for it.

Refund processing and any licence-record correction are separate operating steps; this policy does not promise automatic revocation when Paddle sends a notification. Do not continue using refunded Pro rights.
