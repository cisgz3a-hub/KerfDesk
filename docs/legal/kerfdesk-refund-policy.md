## Try before you buy

Every device can try all Pro features free for 30 days. No payment card is needed. Please use the
trial to check that KerfDesk works with your machine and suits your work before you buy.

## 14 days, no reason needed

You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying a KerfDesk Pro licence or an update extension.
You do not need to tell us why.

## After 14 days

We do not refund because you changed your mind after 14 days. We still refund:

- where the law requires it;
- if you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount; or
- if your licence will not work and we cannot fix the problem within a reasonable time.

## How to ask

Use the link in the receipt Paddle emailed you, or contact us through
https://kerfdesk.com/support.html. Include your Paddle order number if you have it.

Paddle, our reseller and the merchant of record for every order, processes refunds. Paddle refunds
the full amount, including any tax you paid, to your original payment method. How long the money
takes to reach you depends on your bank.

## After a refund

- A refund ends your Pro licence. A refund of an update extension removes that extra year.
- Please deactivate the licence on every device (in the desktop app: Help > Licence, then
  **Deactivate this device**) and stop using the Pro features it unlocked. Activated licences also
  work offline, so we rely on you to do this. We may block a refunded licence key.
- Free keeps working, as it does for everyone.

## Payment disputes

If something is wrong with a payment, please contact us before you dispute it with your bank or
card issuer. It is usually faster. While a dispute is open, we may suspend the licence. If the
payment is reversed, the licence ends as if it had been refunded.

## Fair use

We may refuse a refund where there is clear evidence of abuse, such as repeatedly buying and
refunding licences, unless the law requires the refund.

## Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights under the law where you live. It does not reduce them.
