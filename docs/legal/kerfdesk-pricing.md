## What KerfDesk is

KerfDesk is software for designing jobs for laser cutters, laser engravers and CNC routers, and for running those jobs on your machine over a USB cable. It is built for GRBL-family controllers (GRBL 1.1, grblHAL and FluidNC). It also has drivers for Marlin and Smoothieware lasers, and an experimental file export for Ruida controllers. It comes in two editions: Free and Pro.

## KerfDesk Free

**Free, with no time limit.** No account, no card and no device limit. You may use it for personal or business work.

- Drawing and text
- File import: SVG, DXF, PNG and JPG files, fonts and LightBurn projects
- Basic tracing (the outline presets)
- Laser cutting and engraving
- 2D CNC cuts: profile, pocket, engrave, inlay and drill
- All machine control

Use it in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc) or on the desktop. [Open the app](https://kerfdesk.com)

## KerfDesk Pro (not on sale yet)

Everything in Free, plus these tools:

- **V-carve:** carve lettering and artwork with a V-bit, cutting deeper where shapes are wider.
- **3D relief:** rough and finish 3D reliefs from height maps and models.
- **Adaptive clearing:** clear pockets with a constant tool load, for faster, gentler cuts.
- **Advanced tracing:** centerline, colour-layer, photo-shading and multi-file tracing.
- **Camera alignment:** calibrate the camera lens and line its picture up with your machine bed.
- **Box generator:** make finger-jointed boxes sized to your material.
- **Design Studio:** draw parts to size in a full window, with precision tools, snapping and dimensions.
- **G-code Inspector:** open any G-code file, or your project's own, and check it move by move in 3D.

When Pro goes on sale, it will work only in **KerfDesk for Windows**, the desktop app for Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64). It won't be available in the web app, on a Mac or on Linux.

## How a Pro licence works

- **Pay once, use with no time limit.** A Pro licence doesn't expire. You can keep using every version of KerfDesk for Windows released on or before your update end date, with all its Pro tools, for as long as you like, on the computers you have activated. You can reinstall those versions too. If a computer needs activating again, for example after Windows is reinstalled, that needs our licensing service; section 12.6 of the terms says what we will do if it ever stops. A licence can end only as the terms say, for example after a refund, a reversed payment or a serious breach. We can't promise that older versions will keep running on future versions of Windows.
- **One year of updates.** Your update period starts when Paddle confirms your payment and ends exactly one year later. The app shows it in Help > Licence as “Updates through” a date. On a licensed computer, automatic updates install only versions your licence covers, so an update never takes your Pro tools away. If you install a version released after your update end date yourself, it opens with its Pro tools locked, and everything in Free keeps working.
- **More updates if you want them.** You can buy another year of updates for US$20 plus tax at any time. It is added to your current update end date or, if that date has passed, runs from the day you pay. It also covers versions released while your updates had lapsed, up to the new end date. It is a single payment: it isn't a subscription and never renews automatically.
- **Downloads.** You can download the versions your licence covers again from our download page, for as long as we run it. Keep a copy of any installer you rely on.
- **Up to 3 computers at a time.** Each Windows installation you activate counts as one computer. Installing KerfDesk twice in the same Windows installation counts once. Reinstalling Windows counts as a new computer, so deactivate first. To move your licence, choose Help > Licence > **Deactivate this device** on the old computer, then activate the new one. You can free up to six computers in any 30 days. If a computer is lost or broken, contact us, and we'll free it once we have checked that the request is genuine.
- **Online once, then offline.** Activating needs an internet connection once. After that, KerfDesk works offline. When your computer is online, KerfDesk confirms your licence with our licensing service about once a week, for example to pick up an extra year of updates.
- **Your work keeps running.** Your licence status never blocks machine control or stops a job that is running. If a trial or update period ends, projects that already use Pro operations still preview, frame, run and save as G-code. You need Pro only to open a Pro tool or add a Pro operation. We never delete your files.
- **For you or your organisation.** A licence belongs to the person or organisation that buys it. An organisation's staff and contractors may use it for the organisation, within the 3-computer limit.

## Before you buy: what to check

- No machine has been qualified with KerfDesk yet. It has been used for informal jobs on a Creality Falcon A1 Pro and a Neotronics 4040-class laser. Other controllers have been tested only against simulators. See the [Machines page](https://kerfdesk.com/machines/).
- The CNC tools, including the Pro tools V-carve, 3D relief and adaptive clearing, and photo engraving have been tested in software but haven't yet run on a real machine. Air-cut and test on scrap first.
- Ruida export is experimental: no Ruida controller has accepted a file yet.
- KerfDesk connects to machines over USB only, not over Wi-Fi or a network. Trocen, TopWisdom and galvo controllers aren't supported.
- Use the free trial to check KerfDesk with your own machine before you buy.

**Safety.** Lasers and CNC routers can start fires and cause serious injury, including permanent eye damage. Stay with your machine while it runs. KerfDesk's Abort button is a software stop, not an emergency stop, so keep your machine's own emergency stop within reach. [Read the safety notes](https://kerfdesk.com/safety/).
