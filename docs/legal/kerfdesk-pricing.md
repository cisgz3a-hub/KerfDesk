## KerfDesk Free

Free has no time limit, account, card or computer-seat limit. It runs in the browser and on the desktop and includes drawing/text, file import, Line Art tracing, laser cutting/engraving, 2D CNC cuts and all machine control.

Line Art is the only Free tracer. Every other visible preset, including Sharp, and multi-file tracing are Pro. Browser Free preserves saved Pro projects for the desktop instead of unlocking their operations. The workspace opens as Free without an eligible licence. Licensing never gates Frame, Start, output, Save G-code or a running job.

## KerfDesk Pro for Windows

Everything in Free, plus:

- **V-carve:** carve lettering/artwork with a V-bit.
- **3D relief:** rough and finish height-map/model reliefs.
- **Adaptive clearing:** clear pockets with adaptive toolpaths.
- **Advanced tracing:** Smooth, Sharp, Centerline, Line + fill, Edge Detection, Photo shading, Colour layers and multi-file tracing.
- **Camera alignment:** calibrate and align a camera picture.
- **Box generator:** design finger-jointed boxes.
- **Design Studio:** use the precision design workspace.
- **G-code Inspector:** inspect G-code moves in 3D.

Pro is desktop only, Windows first. Choosing a new Pro tool asks for a trial or eligible licence; existing Pro output and active jobs remain usable.

## Purchase and recovery once checkout opens

1. Choose Buy Pro in Help > Licence in the Windows app, or open https://kerfdesk.com/buy.html in a supported browser, including a phone.
2. Pay through Paddle. Check the total, including any applicable tax, before confirming. Paddle emails your receipt.
3. Select **Check payment** where you started. The Windows app activates its purchase. A browser/phone shows your licence key; save it and enter it later in Help > Licence in Windows. Buying on a phone uses no computer seat.
4. Keep the receipt and save the key. Keep the browser's saved purchase data until you have the key. Private browsing or clearing site data may lose recovery information. The key is not automatically emailed.

Renew an existing paid licence from Help > Licence in Windows. If payment is interrupted/uncertain, resume the saved order or email support@kerfdesk.com with the receipt/order reference. Do not pay twice to resolve uncertainty. Support verifies ownership privately before key recovery.
