KerfDesk prepares and sends instructions to lasers, CNC routers and similar
machines. These machines can cause fire, injury, eye damage and property damage.

- You are responsible for safe machine setup and operation, for supervising every
  job, for eye protection, ventilation and fire safety, and for following your
  machine manufacturer's instructions and local law.
- Always run the Frame check and review a job before starting it. Keep the
  machine's own emergency stop within reach; the software stop in KerfDesk is not
  an emergency stop.
- Material settings, previews, estimates and suggestions are guidance only.
