## ADR-566 - Timed mark at the executable job start

**Status:** Accepted by the maintainer's explicit instruction on 2026-10-02. Software/controller-model verification is separate from material and physical-machine qualification.

**Context.** The maintainer requested a low-power, approximately one-second dot on the artwork showing where its next burn actually starts. Existing held Fire lights the current head position until released. The nine-point Job Origin grid aligns artwork bounds; it does not identify a contour seam, the first nonblank raster pixel, or the first powered motion of the final executable program.

**Decision.** Add a discoverable **Mark job start · 1 s** control for Cartesian laser jobs on GRBL v1.1, grblHAL and FluidNC. Preserve the existing local Labs, machine-profile and controller-capability opt-ins. A disabled control explains the required opt-in or factual busy condition. Marking adds no licence or ordinary Start policy gate. Unsupported firmware, rotary jobs, unknown coordinates and active controller/motion/job transactions remain refused by their existing factual contracts.

One click prepares the current exact output scope and placement through the ordinary owned G54/report-units boundary. The target is `firstProcessPoint` from the final executable motion manifest, excluding S0 motion, blank raster pixels, overscan travel, and geometry omitted by final rounding. Capture the resolved origin and the initial reported head before temporary motion; Current Position is not resolved again at the target. No Frame or Start permit is minted or consumed by a mark.

The owned transaction sends M5, drains the planner, and confirms fresh same-session Idle and the original XYZ. The acknowledged preflight line flushes old realtime flags before a standalone 100% feed/rapid/power override baseline. Explicit G21/G90/G54/G94 beam-off G1 travel goes to the emitted work point without applying placement, mirrors or WCO twice. Its planner drain ACK and a separate fresh Idle/XYZ report are prerequisites to the pulse. Long travel and return drains use an activity-based silence deadline with guarded transaction-owned realtime polling; Run activity cannot substitute for their terminal ACK or arrival evidence.

The pulse is one small transport write containing stationary `G1 F... M3 S...`, `G4 P1`, and `M5`. The controller already has its shutoff behind the one-second dwell; host UI timing is not the sole off mechanism. All three terminal ACKs must settle before beam-off return, an acknowledged planner drain, and fresh same-session Idle at the original observed XYZ. A host watchdog is containment of communication uncertainty, not a measurement of optical exposure.

Use the default 1% commanded positioning share, bounded by the profile consent and existing hard 5% cap. When current-session controller settings prove a smaller positive S ceiling than the saved profile, use that smaller ceiling. Recheck it before the pulse and never increase the initially admitted S. This bounds a commanded S value; it guarantees neither optical watts nor a visible/material-safe dot.

Preparation and execution reserve the existing controller arbiter, so concurrent Start, Frame, Fire, console, override and setup writes cannot overlap. Ordinary held-Fire global release events do not release a mark they never held. An explicit generic Laser off during a mark uses the bound Abort lifecycle instead of inserting an unowned M5 ACK into its three-ACK exchange. Abort, rejection, partial delivery, silence, unexpected controller state, origin drift or replacement session prevents retention. Cleanup belongs to the captured session and cannot send a late M5, return or reset to a replacement controller.

A clean completed ordinary Frame may remain in place through this privately owned temporary excursion. Its session, origin, spatial signature and proof object remain strict; only the known owner's intermediate head/Run observations are exempted. Success requires return to the same observed XYZ. Generic expired evidence is never restored, rebound or recreated. Start remains unavailable while preparation or execution owns the controller, then keeps ADR-565's unchanged-footprint contract.

An optional **Line burn start near** nine-region preference selects an eligible Line entry near the chosen physical artwork region while retaining layer/inside-first and path-direction priorities. Legacy start-point planning remains intact; raster/fill/CNC retain their existing order. A preferred bounds region is neither a promise of an exact contour point nor the dot target. The dot always follows the first powered point of the final output, including unchanged fill and raster scan order. The mark remains laser-only.

**Primary controller evidence.** [GRBL laser-mode documentation](https://github.com/gnea/grbl/wiki/Grbl-v1.1-Laser-Mode) documents stationary M3, stationary-off M4 and the G1/G2/G3 modal requirement. [GRBL dwell implementation](https://github.com/gnea/grbl/blob/bfb67f0c7963fe3ce4aaf8a97f9009ea5a8db36e/grbl/motion_control.c#L174-L180) drains motion then delays in seconds. [grblHAL dwell implementation](https://github.com/grblHAL/core/blob/d7aaee3d84b1e7010f075d395206afff038d7379/motion_control.c#L770-L777) also accepts seconds. [FluidNC G-code dwell handling](https://github.com/bdring/FluidNC/blob/fdc17a2c/FluidNC/src/GCode.cpp#L1818-L1820) converts P seconds to its millisecond dwell. These are the admitted driver families; Marlin and Smoothieware semantics are not assumed.

**Verification required.** Independent final-wire coordinate and modal-power checks; blank/zero-power/collapsed/raster/arc cases; Current Position and nonzero WCO; saved S1000 versus observed S255; physically timed long travel/return and inherited overrides; all-three-ACK ownership; synchronous query response before write completion; deferred preparation and concurrent Start/Frame; mounted Fire release events; refusal, partial writes, silence, Abort, replacement session, origin/head drift and one-way Frame expiry. No real controller or laser is operated by this verification.
