## ADR-567 Amendment 1 - A small whole-number $30 keeps every power, and an empty S range is named (2026-10-21)

**Status:** Accepted under the maintainer's standing authorisation to repair confirmed audit findings (rolling audit v2, findings 03-P2-4 and 03-P2-5, 2026-10-21). It amends ADR-567 for fresh GRBL-family laser output and its Job Review controller warnings. Software evidence does not qualify physical or optical output.

**Context.** ADR-567 gave a fractional $30 a virtual integer range so sub-unit maxima cannot collapse every pixel to zero or one, and kept ordinary rounding for every whole-number range. A small whole-number $30 collapses the same way. At $30=1, a 30% Line was emitted as S0 and a 50% or 70% Line as S1, which is full power, while Job Review showed the set percentage. A 30% grayscale image compiled to all-S0 pixels and was refused as producing no cuts; a 70% image burned at S1. Stock GRBL accepts decimal S words and maps them linearly, so this was KerfDesk's rounding, not the firmware's.

Separately, settings parsing dropped a reported $30=0 as if it were missing, so readiness said only that $30 was not confirmed, and $31 at or above $30 got only "Low nonzero power values may burn hotter". Stock GRBL's [`spindle_compute_pwm_value`](https://github.com/gnea/grbl/blob/bfb67f0c7963fe3ce4aaf8a97f9009ea5a8db36e/grbl/spindle_control.c#L192-L205) returns the maximum PWM when `rpm_min >= rpm_max`, before its S0 branch, so with either setting every S, S0 included, drives full PWM while M3 or M4 is on. That includes S0 laser-off moves and the `M4 S0` preamble. grblHAL computes it differently: its [`spindle_precompute_pwm_values`](https://github.com/grblHAL/core/blob/4ec1ae44b857a70d2f9298344feac4b43ed0eb6b/spindle_control.c#L1045-L1078) marks the spindle as not variable when `rpm_max <= rpm_min`, its PWM conversion then returns the off value, and the driver reverts to on/off spindle control. FluidNC reports only $30, the top of its spindle speed_map.

**Decision.**

1. **Power-scale version 3.** Fresh prepared output records power-scale version 3. It keeps version 2's fractional ranges and decimal S spelling, and also gives a whole-number GRBL-family S maximum below 100 the same virtual range: vector power is the percentage, at 0.1% resolution, times the maximum, and 100% is exactly the maximum. Raster pixels are quantized in the 0..1000 virtual range and rescaled to the controller's S units, kept to twelve significant digits as vector S words are, so 937 of 1000 at $30=20 is S18.74 rather than S18.740000000000002. At $30=1, 30%, 50% and 70% become S0.3, S0.5 and S0.7.

2. **Why 100.** With 100 or more whole S steps every whole percentage lands within half a percentage point of full scale. Fewer steps cannot keep that, and below about 20 steps percentages collapse to S0 or full power. Ranges of 100 or more, including the usual $30=1000 and $30=255, keep whole-S rounding and their exact existing bytes.

3. **Archives keep their meaning.** Version 1 and version 2 records keep their original rounding and raw rescaled raster samples through hydration, resume and second-pass validation, because a version 2 record with a small $30 rebuilds streamed raster rows from its project and must rebuild the same tones. Unknown versions, now anything other than missing, 1, 2 or 3, still fail decode and hydration. Non-GRBL output keeps version 1, as before.

4. **$30=0 is a reported value.** The controller settings snapshot records a reported $30=0 as `maxPowerSReportedAsZero`. `maxPowerS` keeps only a positive range, so a zero top never reaches a profile, the effective power scale or a positioning cap, and output keeps the disclosed profile assumption. Readiness no longer calls a reported zero "not confirmed", and the Job Review `S max $30` fact shows 0 rather than Unknown. A CNC $30=0 is reported as a spindle-scale mismatch rather than a missing setting.

5. **Named warning, never a Start guard.** When the reported $30 is not above $31 (an unreported $31 counts as 0, since GRBL-family settings reject negatives), laser readiness adds the `power-range-empty` Job Review warning in place of the "may burn hotter" advice. Its wording follows the connected firmware, as its banner identifies it, or else the connection driver: for stock GRBL it says that the controller fires full power even at S0, so laser-off moves fire the beam at full power while M3 or M4 is on; for grblHAL that S no longer sets laser power under on/off spindle control; for FluidNC that $30 comes from the spindle's speed_map; and when the firmware is not known, both the stock GRBL and grblHAL facts. Each asks for $30 above $31 before burning. Under ADR-565 this is a policy finding: it never refuses Start, Save or Frame.

**Unchanged.** The connected-$30 binding, Frame reuse, frozen recovery bytes and the ADR-566 positioning controls are as ADR-567 states. CNC spindle output is unchanged.

**Verification.** `core/output/small-whole-number-power-range.test.ts` covers vector and raster S at $30=1, 2, 20 and 99, unchanged bytes and raster values at $30=255 and $30=1000, and versions 1 and 2 keeping their rounding. `ui/laser/recovery-artifact-power-version.audit.test.ts` rehydrates a $30=20 streamed raster at versions 2 and 3 with unchanged bytes and rejects version 4. `core/preflight/controller-readiness-power-range.test.ts` covers the zero report, the empty-range test, the per-firmware wording and the CNC mismatch. The existing G-code snapshots are unchanged. Hardware, optics and firmware qualification remain separate.
