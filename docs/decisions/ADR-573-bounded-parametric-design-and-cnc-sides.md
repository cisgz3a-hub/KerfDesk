## ADR-573 - Bounded parametric design, CNC sides and qualified wrapping

**Status:** Implemented locally under the maintainer's CNC leader audit build request on 2026-10-08. Rotary execution remains unqualified.

Retain editable 2D sketch constraints and bounded common-part generator definitions beside materialized vector artwork. They share the existing scene, operation bindings, overrides, save format and undo history. They do not introduce a solid CAD kernel or a feature timeline. Named parameters retain explicit units and references; contradictory constraints return reviewable residuals without committing replacement geometry. Under-constrained geometry retains free motion. Bake deliberately removes editable intent while preserving the current paths. Applying a regenerated preview requires ownership of the exact reviewed source.

Schema 15 protects these source contracts along with scalar relief intent. Manual path changes remain visible geometry; retained design editors must disclose a materialized mismatch and offer reviewed reapply or bake rather than silently replace those edits. Regeneration preserves machining settings and tabs through uniquely recoverable semantic path identity. Missing, changed or ambiguous materialized paths require Undo or Bake before a replacement preview can be prepared; ordinary artwork and output remain usable. A vector retains only one geometry authority among sketches, generated parts and Boolean compounds.

CNC sides name the active A/B artwork set, physical stock flip axis, registration guides and side B stock origin in G54. Side A uses the canonical stock datum. Each side has stock-top Z0, established separately by the operator. Flipping around stock X reflects Y; flipping around stock Y reflects X. The affine source transform is applied before CAM so cutter winding, offsets, tabs, ramps and relief contact are regenerated in the correct frame. Authored artwork and machine setup remain immutable during preparation. Side B stock placement exists only in the prepared execution copy. Each artifact identifies its side; Frame continues to follow footprint and placement under ADR-565.

CNC wrapping has its own capability record, cylinder datum and units. Laser rotary configuration never enables router rotary motion. Implemented coordinate mappings and offline post simulation are not physical qualification. Unsupported controller/post combinations remain unavailable. Additional WCS, ATC, arbitrary vendor posts and simultaneous axes require separate qualified capabilities. Output selection and existing jobs acquire no new account or licence gates.

Verify analytical dimensions, solver conflicts, stable bindings and IDs, save/reopen, atomic undo, stale preview ownership, both independent flip axes and all device origin frames. Record exact program identity and separate local software, controller simulation and physical-machine evidence.
