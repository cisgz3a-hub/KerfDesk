## ADR-497 Amendment 1 - A Material Test card burns the spacing it names, in readable labels, gentlest cell first (2026-10-23)

**Status:** Accepted under the maintainer's standing authorisation to repair confirmed audit findings (controller audit v4, findings E5-P2-1, E5-P2-2 and E5-P2-3, confirmed by an independent refuter and the coordinator, 2026-10-23). It amends ADR-497 decisions 3, 6 and 7 and the Material Test generator of ADR-044. The same change takes two small findings from the report: the runway note (E5-P2-4, lowered to P3 by the refuter) and float noise in axis values (E5-P3-3). Software evidence does not qualify physical or optical output.

**Context.**

- **Hatch spacing below the fill floor.** The dialog accepted hatch spacing from 0.01 mm. The laser fill plans every hatch at no less than 0.05 mm (`MIN_HATCH_SPACING_MM` in `fill-hatching.ts`), and that floor was recorded only in a code comment. A row labelled 0.01 to 0.04 burned at 0.05 mm, while the card, the dialog preview, the Experiment record and Job Review's override text all named the smaller value. The Interval Test, the scan-offset coupon and Cut Settings' Line interval already stop at 0.05.
- **Column labels overlapped.** Column labels took their size from the cell alone. On the default 10 × 10 grid (5 mm cells, 1 mm gap) a 5-character label such as "23.33" is 7.14 mm wide on a 6 mm pitch, so "23.33"/"26.67" and "33.33"/"36.67" drew digits 0.12 mm apart, where they fuse into no valid digit. 4-digit speed columns overlapped by 0.30 mm, and hatch-spacing and 0–100 power columns overlapped too. The Interval Test already shrinks each label to fit its swatch.
- **Cut grids cut hottest first on some setups.** A row's cells are one Line operation with one group per cell, listed lowest risk first. The cut planner re-plans one operation's groups by distance from the head. On a rear-right, front-right or centre origin (the catalogue's Generic Ruida CO2 profile is rear-right), and on a front-left machine with a right-to-left Cut direction, a front-right Line start region or a job-centre start point, each row was cut hottest cell first. Engraved grids keep their order, because scanline groups are never reordered.
- **The runway note.** When the fastest engraved cell needs more than the 25 mm a row's runway can be (ADR-238 Amendment 3, ADR-495), the dialog still said the 25 mm reached that speed. At 500 mm/s², 25 mm is enough for about 9,040 mm/min with automatic overscan's 10% margin.

**Decision.**

1. **One hatch floor.** `fill-hatching.ts` exports `MIN_HATCH_SPACING_MM` (0.05 mm), and the Material Test, Interval Test, scan-offset coupon and fill estimate read it instead of keeping their own copies. The Material Test's lowest hatch spacing (`MIN_TEST_INTERVAL_MM`) is that floor, so the dialog asks for 0.05 or more and the generator raises a lower value from a preset or another caller before it labels a row. A saved draft below 0.05 shows the dialog's usual range message until it is changed. LightBurn's Material Test documents no lower limit, but a label must name what KerfDesk burns (ADR-044).
2. **Column labels fit the column pitch.** Every column label on a card takes one size: the row label size, or smaller where the widest column label would otherwise take more than 90% of the column pitch (cell width plus gap). This is the Interval Test's fit (`calibrationLabelSizeToFit`, now shared), applied to the pitch. The clear space between two labels is then at least a tenth of the pitch, wider than the space between two digits of one label. Each label is centred over its cell and sits on the cells' edge of the top gutter. The left gutter widens, if needed, so the first column's label stays right of the grid's origin. Row labels are unchanged; they already never touch. Labels are not rotated, so the card reads the same way round as before.
3. **Each Cut cell is planned on its own (decision 3).** The compiler gives each Line group made only of generated Material Test cells (objects whose `source` is `material-test-grid`) its own topology scope. The cut planner then cannot reorder one cell against another, so every row is cut in the generated, lowest-risk-first order on every origin and under every Cut direction, Line start region and start point. Within a cell, the planner still chooses the start point and pass order as before. Other artwork on the same operation keeps the shared scope and its ordinary planning. Grids saved before this change are cut in order too, because their cells carry the same source.
4. **The runway note says when the cap binds (decision 6).** When the fastest cell needs more than 25 mm, the note says that a row's runway cannot be longer, gives the speed 25 mm is enough for at Machine Setup's acceleration, and says faster cells may burn below their labelled speed. The cap itself is unchanged.
5. **Axis values keep six decimals**, as the Interval Test's do, so a cell's spacing is 0.03 rather than 0.030000000000000002 in comments and the Experiment record. Emitted S and F words are unchanged.

**Unchanged.** Burned values, the emitted S, F and pass counts, engraved-grid order, row labels, the 25 mm runway cap and the Interval Test's own labels are as before. The card still names neither axis nor the settings held fixed (E5-P3-2); that needs letters in the label font and is left for later.

**Verification.**

- `src/core/job/material-test-label-bounds.test.ts` checks that no two labels touch (clear space of at least one digit space) and that no label sits on a cell or left of the origin. It covers the default grid, 4- and 5-digit speed columns, hatch-spacing columns, 0–100 power, a gap of 0 and a small-cell Cut grid. It also checks that the default column labels share one size, are centred over their cells, and use the 5.4 mm the 6 mm pitch allows. All of these fail on the previous layout.
- `src/io/gcode/material-test-emitted.test.ts` reads the emitted program back. Hatch rows down to the floor burn at their labelled spacing. A Cut grid is cut in generated order on front-left, rear-right, front-right and centre origins, with right-to-left and bottom-to-top Cut directions, a front-right Line start region and a job-centre start point. Before the change, eight of its ten cases failed.
- `src/ui/calibration/MaterialTestDialog.axes.test.tsx` covers the 0.05 floor in the dialog and the capped-runway note.
- Not tried on a machine.
