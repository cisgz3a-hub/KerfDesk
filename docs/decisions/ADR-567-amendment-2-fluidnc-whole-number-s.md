## ADR-567 Amendment 2 - FluidNC gets whole S, and Job Review names the power steps a small range loses (2026-10-23)

**Status:** Accepted under the maintainer's standing authorisation to repair confirmed audit findings (controller audit v4, finding E1-P2-1, confirmed by an independent refuter and the coordinator, 2026-10-22). It amends ADR-567 and its Amendment 1 for fresh FluidNC laser output and the Job Review power-scale warnings. The same change takes the report's two small P3 findings, E1-P3-2 and E1-P3-3. Software evidence does not qualify physical or optical output.

**Context.** Amendment 1 gave every GRBL-family S maximum below 100 the virtual fractional range, on the ground that the firmware "accepts decimal S words and maps them linearly". That holds for stock GRBL and grblHAL, which keep S as a float. It does not hold for FluidNC. FluidNC parses S as a float, but `SpindleDatatypes.h` declares `typedef uint32_t SpindleSpeed` (main and v4.0.3), and `GCode.cpp` assigns the parsed value to the planner's `SpindleSpeed` without rounding (`pl_data->spindle_speed = gc_state.spindle_speed`). M3 and M4 enable casts it the same way (`(uint32_t)gc_block.values.s`), and M4's rate adjustment starts from the already whole block value. So FluidNC truncates every S toward zero before `Spindle::mapSpeed` maps it through the `speed_map`, whose top is FluidNC's `$30` (`SettingsDefinitions.cpp`, `INT_PROXY("30", …, spindle->maxSpeed())`). With a hand-written `speed_map` top of 1, a 60% Line went out as `S0.6`, ran as S0 and burned nothing. Every power below 100% did the same. At a top of 20, the 34.9% grey of a 60%/10% image went out as `S6.98` and ran at 30%. Job Review showed the set percentages and gave no warning. The usual tops (100, 255, 1000, 10000) and FluidNC's Laser default (`0=0% 255=100%`) were unaffected.

Sources: [SpindleDatatypes.h](https://github.com/bdring/FluidNC/blob/v4.0.3/FluidNC/src/SpindleDatatypes.h), [GCode.cpp](https://github.com/bdring/FluidNC/blob/main/FluidNC/src/GCode.cpp), [Spindles/Spindle.cpp](https://github.com/bdring/FluidNC/blob/main/FluidNC/src/Spindles/Spindle.cpp), [SettingsDefinitions.cpp](https://github.com/bdring/FluidNC/blob/main/FluidNC/src/SettingsDefinitions.cpp).

**Decision.**

1. **Power-scale version 4.** Fresh prepared output records power-scale version 4. Under it a FluidNC profile uses version 1's whole-S rounding for vector power, raster quantization and S spelling, as non-GRBL dialects already do: each power is `round(percent × $30)`, and raster pixels are quantized in the whole `0..$30` range. GRBL v1.1 and grblHAL keep version 3's virtual range unchanged. For a FluidNC range of 100 or more the bytes are the same as under version 3.

2. **Archives keep their meaning.** Version 2 and 3 records keep their fractional FluidNC bytes through hydration, resume and second-pass validation, as Amendment 1 point 3 requires. Those bytes are what was sent and reviewed. Version 4 joins the known versions, and anything other than missing, 1, 2, 3 or 4 still fails decode and hydration.

3. **What reaches the controller.** `vectorPowerReachesController` uses the same per-device version, so a FluidNC power that rounds to S0 no longer counts as reaching the controller. The cutting-start bounds, the job start point and laser leads leave it out, as they do for any S0 group. Frame keeps its full motion envelope.

4. **Job Review names the lost steps.** When a FluidNC job's full-power S is below 100, the power-scale warnings that Save, Preview and Start review show gain a `fluidncPowerStepWarning`. It uses the range the bytes are written for: the connected controller's `$30` when ADR-567 binds it, otherwise the saved profile. It says that FluidNC keeps S whole and gives the step size, how far a power can land from its set value, and the power below which S0 is sent and nothing burns. At a top of 1 it says instead that every power below 50% burns nothing and every power from 50% burns at full power. It asks for a `speed_map` top of 100 or more. Under ADR-565 this is a policy finding: it never refuses Start, Save or Frame.

5. **The raised-`$31` advice follows the firmware (E1-P3-2).** The `min-power-nonzero` warning said that low powers "may burn hotter". Stock GRBL does the opposite. Its `spindle_compute_pwm_value` maps S linearly from `$31` up to `$30` and gives any S above 0 at or below `$31` the lowest PWM step, so with `$31=100` and `$30=1000`, 60% burns at 55.7%. The warning now says that every power below 100% burns weaker than set, gives the 50% example and the share at or below `$31` from the reported values, and asks for `$31=0`. For grblHAL it names `$35`: grblHAL maps the same way with `$35` as its PWM floor, and a raised `$35` lifts low powers instead. When the firmware is not known it gives both facts.

6. **A Marlin S above one byte is named (E1-P3-3).** Stock Marlin laser builds (`CUTTER_POWER_UNIT` PWM255 or PERCENT) declare `cutter_power_t` as `uint8_t`, and `gcode.cpp` narrows the float S into it before `power_to_range` can clamp it, so S600 runs as 88 and S1000 as 232 ([spindle_laser_types.h](https://github.com/MarlinFirmware/Marlin/blob/2.1.2.5/Marlin/src/feature/spindle_laser_types.h)). When a Marlin inline profile's Full-power S is above 255, the machine job warnings gain `marlinPowerScaleWarning`, beside the Smoothieware twin. It says what 100% wraps to on a PWM255 build, asks for 255 (PWM255) or 100 (PERCENT), and keeps the value only for an RPM build whose `SPEED_POWER_MAX` matches. Fan wiring always writes 0..255 and gets no warning. Saved values are never migrated.

**Unchanged.** The connected-`$30` binding, Frame reuse, frozen recovery bytes, the ADR-566 positioning controls and Amendment 1's `power-range-empty` warning stay as they are. CNC spindle output is unchanged. A GRBL profile driving a FluidNC board still gets version 3 bytes. The existing controller-identity warning already asks the operator to choose FluidNC for that machine.

**Verification.** `src/__fixtures__/controllers/fluidnc-laser-power-model.ts` is an independent FluidNC power model: it drops the S fraction, then maps through a `speed_map`. `core/output/fluidnc-whole-number-power.test.ts` judges FluidNC bytes with it at `$30` of 1, 20 and 255. It covers vector Lines, a 60%/10% grayscale image, the version 3 bytes that truncated, unchanged `$30=255` bytes, the S0 reach check, and a connected `$30=20` through `prepareOutput` with a version 3 record that keeps `S6.6`. `core/devices/fluidnc-power-scale.test.ts`, `core/devices/marlin-power-scale.test.ts`, `ui/laser/power-scale-controller-warnings.test.ts` and `core/preflight/controller-readiness-power-range.test.ts` cover the warning texts and where they appear. Hardware, optics and firmware qualification remain separate.
