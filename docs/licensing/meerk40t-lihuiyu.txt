Original files: meerk40t/lihuiyu/laserspeed.py, driver.py, controller.py,
parser.py, device.py; meerk40t/ch341/libusb.py, mock.py
KerfDesk files: src/core/controllers/lihuiyu/ (lhy-speed-code.ts,
lhy-distance.ts, lhy-packet.ts, lhy-writer.ts, lhy-link.ts),
src/__fixtures__/controllers/lihuiyu-decoder.ts, lihuiyu-simulator.ts
Carried over: the speed-code equations and their measured constants
(784/896/1024, 5120/5632/6144, 12120, 2000, the 0.261199033289 diagonal
ratio, the acceleration bands and the suffix-C cut-off), the distance letter
encoding, the packet framing rules and status codes, and the parser's state
diagram. Changes: reimplemented in TypeScript for this app's prepared jobs;
the CRC-8 is computed bitwise rather than with MeerK40t's lookup table; image
rows run as program-mode sweeps rather than MeerK40t's raster mode.
