Ostromoukhov variable-coefficient error-diffusion weights

Source: Victor Ostromoukhov, "A Simple and Efficient Error-Diffusion
Algorithm", Proceedings of SIGGRAPH 2001, pp. 567-572,
https://doi.org/10.1145/383259.383326
Author's copy: https://www.iro.umontreal.ca/~ostrom/varcoeffED/SIGGRAPH01_varcoeffED.pdf
Material used: the integer error-filter weights printed in the paper's
Appendix I for input levels 0..127, transcribed into
src/core/raster/dither-blue-noise-tables.ts (ADR-649).
KerfDesk files: src/core/raster/dither-blue-noise-tables.ts,
src/core/raster/dither-blue-noise.ts
Changes: rows used only up to KerfDesk's first re-optimised key level; the
diffusion, threshold modulation and midtone weights are KerfDesk's own
implementation. No upstream source code is included.

Status: the paper states, "Our algorithm has been developed in an academic
framework. It is our firm intention to put it into the public domain." No
separate licence accompanies the published table. It is reproduced here with
attribution to its author.
