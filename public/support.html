KerfDesk support

Help with the KerfDesk web app and desktop app.

Reporting a problem

If a machine does anything unexpected, stop it with the machine's own emergency stop first. Abort in KerfDesk is a software stop, not an emergency stop.

Then choose Help > Save Support Report in KerfDesk and attach the file it saves. It lists your KerfDesk version, your computer, your machine and its controller, the machine console and the app's recent problems, and in the desktop app its log. It never includes your licence key. Open it first to see exactly what you are sending.

Also tell us:

  1. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  2. The project file, if you are happy to share it.

If your KerfDesk has no Save Support Report, or will not open, include instead:

  1. Your KerfDesk version, from Help > About KerfDesk.
  2. Whether you use the web app or the desktop app, and your operating system.
  3. Your machine and its controller (for example GRBL 1.1 or grblHAL).
  4. If the desktop app will not open, its log files: on Windows the logs folder in %APPDATA%\laserforge, and on a Mac the logs folder in ~/Library/Application Support/laserforge.

Never include your licence key, passwords or payment details. KerfDesk support will never ask for them.
