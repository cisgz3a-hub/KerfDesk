KerfDesk Refund Policy

Version 1.2. Published: 10 October 2026.

This is our refund policy for a KerfDesk Pro or optional update purchase through Paddle. It applies to purchases made on or after 10 October 2026. Purchase availability is shown on the KerfDesk purchase page. Publishing this policy does not itself open sales or create a charge.

Please try before you buy. Every Windows installation can use all Pro tools free for 30 days, with no payment card. Use the trial to check that KerfDesk works with your machine, controller and materials before you purchase.

1. Refund requests

To request a refund for a recent purchase, email support@kerfdesk.com within 14 calendar days after purchase. This applies to the initial Pro purchase and to an optional year of updates.

We consider each refund request on a case-by-case basis and may decline it. We may take into account whether you used the free trial, how Pro has been used, the reason you give and any evidence of fraud, refund abuse or other manipulative behaviour. A refund we choose to give does not commit us to give one for a similar request.

Paddle may also issue refunds under its own discretionary policy. Section 2 lists the rights that apply whatever we decide.

2. Rights that always apply

Section 1 does not limit any of the following, before or after 14 days:

  • a statutory right to withdraw from or cancel the purchase that the law where you live gives you, for example the 14-day withdrawal right of consumers in the EU, EEA, Switzerland and the UK where it has not been waived, exercised through Paddle;
  • a refund or correction of a duplicate or incorrect charge;
  • a remedy when a paid licence is not delivered or cannot be activated, and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time;
  • remedies for a product that is faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose; and
  • any other right or remedy that applicable law gives you.

Paddle’s Refund Policy recognises additional rights supplied by the software supplier and mandatory law. The highest applicable level of rights applies.

3. Purchases made before 10 October 2026

A purchase made before 10 October 2026 keeps the refund promise of version 1.1 of this policy: a full refund if you requested it within 14 calendar days after that purchase, with no reason needed, even after activating and using Pro, unless there was clear evidence of fraud or refund abuse.

KerfDesk is made and licensed by Johannes Stephanus Stolk, a sole proprietor trading as KerfDesk, in South Africa. He is not VAT-registered. Email support@kerfdesk.com for software, licence and refund help; email is our primary support route.

Paddle processes payment, receipts, applicable tax and the return of money. KerfDesk supplies the software and licence and reviews refund requests under this policy. Paddle’s address and identity are separate from our proprietor’s.

Read Paddle’s Buyer Terms and Paddle’s Refund Policy. Nothing in this policy limits a right or remedy that applicable law gives you.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@kerfdesk.com. Tell us that you want a refund, why, and provide the email used for the purchase and, if available, the Paddle receipt or order reference. Never send a full licence key or payment-card details.

You can also use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit paddle.net and choose its refund request option. An eligible statutory withdrawal can also be requested through the options Paddle provides for your transaction.

We verify the purchase and tell you our decision. When we approve a refund, or a right in section 2 applies, we promptly request it from Paddle. Keep the receipt and a copy of your request; a timely request does not become late while it is being considered.

If a purchase appears incomplete, do not pay again to resolve uncertainty. Keep the receipt and recovery information and ask us to check the order.

6. How the money is returned

An approved full refund covers the amount you paid for the refunded purchase, including the applicable tax. Paddle normally returns it to the original payment method where possible. Its published policy normally provides processing within 14 days after approval; statutory withdrawal and other mandatory refund deadlines apply where required.

How long the refund then takes to appear depends on the payment method and bank. Contact us or Paddle if the expected refund has not arrived. We do not replace a refund with store credit unless you choose that and the law permits it.

7. Effect on Pro rights

A full refund of the initial Pro purchase ends the Pro rights granted by that purchase. Free remains available, rights from other valid purchases remain, and no licence or refund action stops a running job.

A refund of an optional update year does not revoke your base Pro licence. We will correct the update coverage where that is supported safely. If we cannot remove the added coverage without taking away your base rights, you keep that extra coverage without being charged for it.

Refund processing and any licence-record correction are separate operating steps; this policy does not promise automatic revocation when Paddle sends a notification. Do not continue using refunded Pro rights.

8. Disputes, policy versions and contact

You may contact us or Paddle about an incorrect charge before raising a payment dispute. This does not affect your right to contact your bank, card issuer or consumer authority, or to use any statutory remedy.

The policy supplied on the day of your purchase applies, unless a later policy gives you greater rights. The version and publication date above identify this policy. Save or print it; ask support@kerfdesk.com for the applicable version if needed.
