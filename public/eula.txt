   You are solely responsible for safe operation, including: never
   leaving a running machine unattended, and watching it directly (a
   camera, phone, or remote connection is not supervision); verifying
   every job (preview, simulation, or air run) before cutting real
   material; not directing the machine to ignite flammable material;
   not letting untrained people or unsupervised children use it;
   switching it off when it is not in use; eye protection rated for
   your laser, ventilation, and fume and dust extraction; keeping a
   suitable fire extinguisher within reach; correct machine
   configuration, work holding, and emergency-stop access; and
   following the machine manufacturer's instructions and applicable
   safety regulations.
