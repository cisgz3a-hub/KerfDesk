10. Machine safety: please read this carefully

KerfDesk prepares and sends instructions to lasers, CNC routers and similar machines. These machines can start fires, cause serious injury, including permanent eye damage, burns and cuts, and damage property. A laser beam, even a reflection, can blind in an instant. Cutting some materials gives off toxic or corrosive fumes. A CNC tool can break or throw material. A mistake in a design, a setting, the software, the connection or the machine can make a machine move or fire when you do not expect it.

You are solely responsible for operating your machine safely, and for the safety of everyone near it. In particular:

  • Never leave a running machine unattended.
  • Check every job before you cut real material: review the preview, run the Frame check, and do an air run when in doubt.
  • Set up and maintain your machine correctly, including its configuration, work holding, enclosure, interlocks, fire safety, ventilation and fume extraction.
  • Wear eye protection rated for your laser's wavelength and power, and protect anyone nearby.
  • Know what you are cutting. Do not cut materials that give off toxic or corrosive fumes.
  • Keep a suitable fire extinguisher and the machine's own emergency stop within reach.
  • The stop controls in KerfDesk, such as Abort, are software stops, not emergency stops. They depend on your computer, the connection and the machine's controller, and they may not stop the machine at once.
  • Follow your machine manufacturer's instructions and the safety laws and rules that apply where you work.
  • Material settings, previews, simulations, estimates, preflight checks and suggestions are aids, not guarantees that a job is correct, safe or suited to your machine and material.
  • KerfDesk is not a safety device. Do not rely on it to prevent harm.

When you accept this agreement, you confirm that you have read this section, that you understand these risks, and that you accept responsibility for operating your machine safely.
