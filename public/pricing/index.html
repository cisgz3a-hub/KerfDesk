How a Pro licence works

  • Pay once. A Pro licence costs US$49.50, one time. It is not a subscription.
  • A year of updates, then yours to keep. Every licence includes one year of updates. Every version released during that year keeps working forever.
  • More updates only if you want them. After the year, US$20 adds another year of updates. It is optional, it is not a subscription and it never renews automatically.
  • Up to 3 devices at a time. Each installation of the desktop app counts as one device. To move the licence, deactivate it on one device, then activate it on another.
  • Try Pro free for 30 days. Once the desktop app is released, each device gets a free 30-day Pro trial. No card needed.
  • Your jobs keep running. A licence never stops a job from running. When a trial ends, only the Pro tools lock, and everything in Free keeps working.
