Pro license

How a Pro license works

  • Pay once

    A Pro license costs US$49.50, one time. It isn’t a subscription.

  • A year of updates, then yours to keep

    Every license includes one year of updates. Every version released during that year keeps working forever.

  • More updates only if you want them

    After the year, US$20 adds another year of updates. It’s optional, it isn’t a subscription and it never renews automatically.

  • Up to 3 devices at a time

    Each installation of the desktop app counts as one device. To move the license, deactivate it on one device, then activate it on another.

  • Try Pro free for 30 days

    Each device gets a free 30-day Pro trial. No card needed.

  • Your jobs keep running

    A license never stops a job from running. When a trial ends, only the Pro tools lock, and everything in Free keeps working.

Questions

About pricing and licenses

Can I buy Pro today?

Yes. Open the KerfDesk purchase page on a computer, phone or tablet, or choose Help > Licence in the Windows app. After paying, return to the same purchase flow and choose Check payment. Save the licence key and your Paddle receipt, then enter the key in Help > Licence on Windows. Buying on a phone does not activate it or use a licence seat.

What if checkout closes or my licence does not appear?

Return in the same browser or Windows app and choose Check payment. Keep the browser’s site data until you have saved your key. If you paid and cannot recover the purchase, email support@kerfdesk.com with your receipt and purchase email. Do not pay again to resolve an uncertain result, and never send a full licence key or card details.

Does the Free edition expire?

No. Free has no time limit, in the browser and on the desktop, and it needs no account or card.

What happens when my trial ends?

Only the Pro tools lock. Everything in Free keeps working, and a license never stops a job from running.

What happens after my year of updates?

Your license keeps working. Every version released during your year of updates keeps working forever. If you want newer versions, US$20 adds another year of updates. It’s optional, it isn’t a subscription and it never renews automatically.

Can I use Pro in the browser?

No. Pro works in the Windows desktop app. KerfDesk in the browser is the Free edition, with no time limit.

How many devices can I use Pro on?

Up to 3 at a time. Each installation of the desktop app counts as one device. To move the license, deactivate it on one device, then activate it on the other. In the desktop app, that’s under Help > Licence.

Do the prices include tax?

No. Prices are in US dollars and exclude any sales tax or VAT the payment provider adds at checkout.

What about refunds?

Please try every Pro tool free for 30 days before you buy. You can request a refund within 14 calendar days after purchase; each request is considered case by case and may be declined. Your statutory rights, and refunds for duplicate charges or an undelivered licence, always apply. See the Refund Policy.

What about the version I have now?

Versions already released keep the terms they were released under. See the license details.
