What these figures mean

These are estimates of installer GET requests reaching dl.kerfdesk.com, including downloads served from Cloudflare's cache. They are not a count of people, completed downloads or installations.

Repeat downloads, update downloads, bots and release checks can contribute. Partial requests are shown separately because a resumed download can make several requests. HEAD requests, manifests, failed responses and unknown file paths are excluded.

Cloudflare can sample traffic and has a limited retention window. Refresh regularly to keep daily aggregate observations on this computer. Missing history cannot be reconstructed. The current day is provisional; saved observations are not an all-time total.

No IP addresses, cookies, device identifiers, licence records or project data are collected by this dashboard. It adds no tracking to the app.
